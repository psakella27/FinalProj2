# FinalProj

This paper examines patterns of demographic targeting in online hate speech
using the a hate speech corpus. Using a comment × target group unit of analysis,
I investigate which groups are most frequently targeted, how the language of hate
speech varies across groups, and how often multiple groups are co-targeted in
the same comment. I find that women are targeted more than twice as often as
the next most common group. Co-targeting is common and follows interpretable
patterns consistent with intersectionality theory: gender and sexuality categories
tend to be attacked together, as do racial and ethnic categories. Specific phrases
such as “go back” and “illegal” map predictably onto immigrant and Latinx targets,
while “terrorist” is disproportionately applied to Middle Eastern, Muslim, and left-
leaning political groups. These findings highlight the structured, socially patterned
nature of online hate speech.
